Changelog

Version history for Scrobble Time Machine

v2.7

2026-10-07 Fix
  • No more stutter: a short hiccup a few seconds into some songs is gone. The radio used to restart playback mid-song when it queued more tracks; now the new tracks are swapped in at the end of the last queued song, so there can be a very short pause between songs at that point instead
  • Right song or none: when a track is not on Spotify, the app no longer plays a similarly named song by another artist. It only accepts a match with the same artist and title, and skips the track otherwise

v2.6

2026-10-06 Feature
  • Artist photo: on wide desktop screens the artist panel now shows a photo of the artist that is playing, next to the bio and your play counts. Click it to open the artist on Spotify
  • Photos come from Spotify: Last.fm no longer provides artist images, so they are loaded from Spotify, once per artist. When there is no photo, a microphone placeholder stays in its place
  • Desktop only: on narrower screens and phones the photo is hidden and nothing extra is requested

v2.5

2026-10-01 Feature
  • New home: the headline, your username and Start radio now sit together in one card, with a larger Start radio button
  • Time Travel cards: the mode pills and the More toggle are replaced by a grid of nine cards, each with a short description, so every mode is visible at once
  • Radio view: now playing sits in a compact 224px card with a larger cover and a bigger play button; on phones the cover goes full width above the controls
  • Wide screens: on large desktop screens the home puts the radio card on the left and the Time Travel modes on the right, and the radio and Time Travel views use two columns: the player and the artist panel on the left, the playlist on the right at the same height (it scrolls inside its panel in Time Travel)
  • Quieter: the green and progress messages are gone; only errors and warnings (for example when Spotify is limiting searches) still show up
  • Steady Up next: the radio keeps the next tracks queued, so the Up next list always shows a full set of rows of the same size
  • Up next: each row now shows the cover, and Time Travel names the moment in the title
  • Back to your radio: Home no longer stops the music. While a radio or Time Travel session is playing, the home card shows what is playing and a Back to radio button, and Start a new radio is still there
  • No more bottom player bar: the radio view already has the controls, so the bar is gone. A volume slider now sits next to the playback controls on wide screens. Save as Playlist is only in Time Travel now, next to the track list
  • Phones: the like heart moves next to the song title so previous, play and next stay centered, and the progress bar now spans the full width of the player

v2.4

2026-09-30 Major
  • Library Radio: one click plays an endless stream of tracks picked at random from your entire Last.fm history, each from a different point in time
  • New home: your scrobble count, one Start radio button, and time travel tucked under its own heading
  • Radio view: now playing with a short artist bio and how many times you have listened to the artist and the song, controls, a clickable Up next list, and Save as Playlist
  • Time Travel: every mode now plays in the same view as the radio, with the artist bio, your play counts, and an Again button
  • No page numbers: moments are described by date instead
  • Gentler on Spotify: Time Travel searches 5 tracks first and loads more as you listen, and it stops and tells you when Spotify asks it to slow down
  • Polish: higher-contrast text in both themes, one consistent corner radius, icons instead of emoji, and reduced-motion support

v2.2

2026-03-26 Major
  • Embedded player — Spotify Web Playback SDK streams audio directly in your browser; no external device required
  • Player bar — persistent bottom bar with album art, track name, artist, prev/play-pause/next controls, seekable progress bar, and volume slider
  • Liked songs — heart button on every track row and in the player bar; checked automatically when each track starts playing; one click to save or remove from your Spotify Liked Songs
  • Save as Playlist — auto-named with mode context (date, artist, decade…), button becomes "Saved! Open" and links directly to the new playlist in Spotify
